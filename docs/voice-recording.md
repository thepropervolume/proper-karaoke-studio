# Record a voice stem

Choose **Record a stem** in **Audio Stems**, then set its role and optional part label. **Start recording** requests access to the default microphone. After **Stop recording**, you can play the take before saving. **Save and add stem** saves a local 48 kHz, 16-bit mono WAV and adds it to the project. Choose a new filename, or cancel the file picker to return to the take. Closing the recorder discards any unsaved take.

Each take starts at the beginning of the project and lasts up to five minutes. Studio pauses preview playback when opening the recorder and keeps microphone input silent in the speakers. Recordings sit beside other audio stems and can be included in video exports and TPV kits.

Enable microphone access for Studio in your system settings if prompted. On macOS, reopen Studio after changing its microphone access. Keep the saved WAV when moving or reopening the project.
